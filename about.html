// ABOUT ME

I hold a bachelor's degree in early childhood education, with a specialization in nature and outdoor education, from Queen Maud University College of Early Childhood Education. I've worked for several years as a kindergarten teacher and lead teacher. In recent years, I've also been responsible for the kindergarten's health, safety and environment (HSE) work, including ICT security.

I've always cared about doing things thoroughly and about protecting people's privacy and security. Technology and ICT have interested me for many years, and that interest led me to study cybersecurity, which I'm now pursuing with real enthusiasm.

Outside work and studies, I recharge with hikes in the forest and the mountains. I feel at home in nature, and I enjoy a quiet night in a tent, fishing in a mountain lake and photographing the landscape around me.

I build and maintain this website myself as a hobby. It's where I practice web development and security, using what I learn through both work and study.

If you have any questions or would like to get in touch, please use the contact form on this website.

// Experience

I started working early, and from lower secondary school until I qualified as an early childhood teacher, I held various jobs alongside my studies. Taking part in important tasks in society has always given me great joy. Along the way, I have gained experience from internships in public administration, residential support work with children and young people, and part-time jobs in retail and customer service.

Since completing my bachelor's degree in early childhood education at Queen Maud University College, I have worked in the kindergarten sector and gained experience in pedagogy, leadership, teamwork, parental guidance and collaboration with external agencies. In recent years, I have taken on greater responsibility for health, safety and environment (HSE) work in the kindergarten, leading risk assessments, emergency preparedness and the development of procedures. I have also been responsible for following up the organisation's IT security, applying the knowledge and skills I continuously gain through my ongoing studies in cybersecurity.
